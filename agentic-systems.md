# Autonomous Systems

Secure systems for coordination and automation. We build architectures where specialized agents work together within strict security boundaries.

---

## Principles

- **Security by Design**: Every component works in isolation to prevent a single failure from spreading.
- **Clear Coordination**: Agents communicate through a central broker to ensure every action is tracked and authorized.
- **User Control**: Sensitive actions require explicit approval.
- **Focus on Clarity**: Systems are designed to explain their reasoning and provide a clear audit trail.
