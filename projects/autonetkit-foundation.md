# Network Modeling Foundations

<span class="status-badge status-active">Active</span>

---

## Concept

The original research that established the principles of automated network configuration. This work introduced the Whiteboard-Plan-Build transformation model, allowing engineers to work with high-level design abstractions while the system handles implementation details.

---

## Research Contribution

- **Abstractions**: identified the fundamental primitives for representing network intent independently of vendor syntax
- **Transformations**: developed graph-based algorithms to automatically calculate IP addresses, OSPF areas, and BGP peerings
- **Scalability**: verified that automated generation handles core-network topologies with hundreds of devices in seconds
- **Industry adoption**: earlier iterations were integrated into industry tooling for automated lab provisioning
